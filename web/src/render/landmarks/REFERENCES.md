# Landmark reference study

Real photographs fetched through web image search and visually inspected on 2026-10-02 PT **before the corresponding builder was authored**. These links are modelling references only; no photographs or textures ship with the models. Local inspection sheets and downloads are in ignored `artifacts/landmarks/references/`.

The renderer uses original procedural geometry, linear vertex colours and metre-scale dimensions. Source photos remain with their respective owners. No image is used as a texture.

## Golden Gate Bridge

Two tall rectangular portal towers; warm vermilion International Orange; suspended deck very thin relative to the 1,280 m main span; close vertical hangers and deep sagging main cables. Use 227.4 m towers and 27.4 m deck width.

## Bay Bridge (western suspension spans)

Four pale steel X-braced towers, two consecutive suspension spans and a solid central concrete anchorage. Preserve the visibly deep double-deck truss, unlike Golden Gate. Grey steel, tan concrete, 160.3 m towers, two 704 m main spans.

1. [Reference 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_bridges_in_the_United_States) · [photograph](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a9/Bay_Bridge.jpg)

## Painted Ladies

Seven adjacent Victorian houses step along a slope; steep front gables, bay windows, white ornamental trim, raised entrances and front stairs. Muted green, ochre, cream and blue-grey facades; dark slate roofs. House dimensions are photo-scale estimates, not survey measurements.

## Ferry Building — spawn landmark

Long pale stone arcaded hall, large four-sided pale clock faces with dark markers/hands, a square clock shaft, columned belfry, stepped green-grey cupola. 245 ft / 74.676 m clock tower, 660 ft / 201 m hall, 22 ft / 6.7 m clock dials. The plaza has palms and a clear central approach; the waterfront side has railed piers and curved white ferry-gate canopies. Plaza/terminal layouts are simplified for walking; the central spawn approach remains open.

## Pier 39

Long boardwalk with a central open route, irregular two-storey clapboard shop rows, pitched grey roofs, pale trim and bright awnings. West-side floating rectangular docks carry clusters of warm brown sea lions with visible flippers. A car-clear centre aisle and turn-around are an intentional game adaptation of the real pedestrian pier.

Each registry entry also links its geographic source. Geographic coordinates use the exact WGS84 → ENU projection and SF_FRAME from `03a0727`, not an independent approximation. Structural heights and terrain elevations are separate; fallback base elevations are temporary placement estimates except Sutro’s documented 254.2 m. Real map samples take precedence.

The Bay Bridge anchor uses the **western span** coordinate, 37°47′53.52″ N, 122°22′40.44″ W, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Western_span_of_the_San_Francisco%E2%80%93Oakland_Bay_Bridge). The frequently quoted whole-bridge coordinate is on the eastern crossing and would misplace this four-tower model. Pier 39's [geographic record](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1856083) locates the model centre; its deck/shops and Painted Ladies heights are explicitly photo-scale estimates (`heightEstimated`), not surveyed dimensions. Lombard's 1.2 m height describes the planting, not the hill.
