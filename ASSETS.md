# Assets omitted from this public snapshot

These were generated with paid or restrictive third-party services and are not redistributed here. They are not CC0. The generators and recipes remain in `tools/art/` and `tools/assets/` so you can reproduce equivalents with your own accounts.

| Omitted path | What it was | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `web/public/assets/avatars/meshy-*.glb` | Rigged player, pedestrian and officer characters | Meshy (paid plan) |
| `web/public/assets/vehicles-pbr/`, `web/public/assets/facades-pbr/`, `web/public/art/facades-pbr/` | PBR vehicles and storefront pieces | Meshy (paid plan) |
| `web/public/art/{props,foliage,billboards}/` | Street-prop textures and billboard art | Scenario |
| `web/public/art/{facades,loading,ground}/` | Facade atlas, loading screens, ground tiles | Scenario, fal (Flux) |
| `web/public/audio/radio/*-track-*.mp3` | 20 generated genre-radio tracks | AI audio generation |
| `tools/art/meshy-ledger.json` | Generation and credit ledger | n/a |

Everything else (code, original synthesized audio loops, CC0 Kenney/Quaternius/Poly Haven assets, and map-derived road data) is documented in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md). Map-derived data is derived from OpenStreetMap (ODbL, attribution required), DataSF (PDDL) and USGS 3DEP LiDAR (public domain).
