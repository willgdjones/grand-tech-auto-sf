# SF procedural landmarks

Open `/landmarks.html` through `npm run dev` to inspect the models. Each builder returns a fresh `THREE.Group` with one merged, flat-shaded vertex-colour mesh and no textures. All geometry uses metres. Source photographs, dimensions and fidelity decisions are in [REFERENCES.md](./REFERENCES.md).

`createLandmark(id, { detail: 'near' | 'far' })` creates a model at its local origin. The ten IDs are `ferry-building`, `transamerica-pyramid`, `salesforce-tower`, `coit-tower`, `sutro-tower`, `golden-gate-bridge`, `bay-bridge`, `painted-ladies`, `lombard-flower-beds`, and `pier-39`. `createPlacedLandmarks({ heightAt })` creates the city group using the map pipeline's exact `SF_FRAME` and WGS84 projection. The city entry checks `flags.landmarks`; the standalone workshop deliberately builds individual assets for inspection. Call `updateLandmarkTerrain(group, heightAt)` after chunks arrive or once per second: it updates base heights and resamples Lombard planting clusters without rebuilding geometry. Structural heights are separate from terrain elevation. See `LANDMARKS` for sourced coordinates, explicit fallback elevations and photo-scale height estimates.

The Ferry Building faces local +z and rotates -53.75631 degrees in the city. Its base aligns the paving to terrain sampled at the plaza arrival, rather than the lower building-centre DEM sample. `ferrySpawn(heightAt)` returns that clear plaza pose facing its clock tower (heading derived from the landmark rotation); `FERRY_SPAWN` is its fallback-height equivalent. The default public spawn remains at its clear Embarcadero plaza point, with heading 64.2977 degrees toward the aligned tower. The hall has a local collision rectangle in `userData.solidFootprints`; the plaza and four rear piers have `userData.walkableSurfaces`. Remove the map's generic Ferry Building massing while retaining its collision footprint so it cannot cover the authored arcade facade.

This puts the authored facade on the surveyed axis, correcting the previous approximate rotation/centre that obstructed both lanes of OSM Embarcadero way `850162148`. Their URLs and observed silhouette, proportions and colour are recorded in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md#ferry-building--spawn-landmark); cached images remain in `artifacts/landmarks/references/ferry-{1..5}.jpg`. The land-facing plaza edge has a four-metre apron, local z=79–83. Its 1m grid blends the plaza height into the loaded road/terrain contact; walking uses the exact rendered triangle interpolation. Supply `world.surfaceHeightAt` to placement and `updateLandmarkTerrain` so geometry and movement choose the same road surface. Missing ground hides the apron until the next terrain update. The transition adds no collider exception or change to the citywide step/cliff limits. `tools/ferry-apron-smoke.mjs` reproduces the original blocked westward walk against source terrain, then checks exit/return and mesh/contact agreement before desktop/phone review.

Apron acceptance (2026-10-03 07:16 UTC): the original boundary stalls all 240 movement steps; the corrected source-ground route exits in 104 steps and returns fully onto the plaza in 123 steps, at most 1.7cm vertical change per step. The four raycast comparisons agree with walking height to below 1e-5m. Desktop and low touch-phone views were inspected with production bloom, zero console/page/world errors. Evidence: `artifacts/ferry-apron/{movement.json,smoke.json,desktop.png,phone.png}`. This scoped proof is not a live-keyboard, performance, physical-device or deployment gate.

The feature can be enabled for local play once the integrator adds the group and map-height updates. Deployed map coverage, physical device acceptance and the later G1/B06/L01 gates remain separate. No external integration is required by these procedural assets.

Pier 39 has a 300 m deck, two detailed shop rows, carousel, west-side K Dock sea lions, and an open north turnaround. Its centre aisle is intentionally widened from the real pedestrian pier for game driving. Click **Drive onto Pier 39** in the workshop; WASD/arrows drive the existing Kenney hybrid using the production driving controller, Space brakes, and touch arrows support phones. Choose an orbit view to stop. `withLandmarkSurfaces(baseMovementWorld, placedGroup)` adds authored plaza/deck heights, shop collisions and pier-edge boundaries to an existing movement world. The pier entrance is local `(0,0,178)`, facing `yaw=Math.PI`; the model rotates +12 degrees in the city, with its deck at 3.2 m above sea level. The apron connects toward the Embarcadero on local +z. Real map coverage at that approach is required for city drive-in acceptance.

`landmarks.test.ts` is a single route smoke: accelerate from the apron, drive the aisle, make a north-end U-turn through the real driving controller, and return to the apron while respecting shops/water. Property, performance and golden tests are deferred under the founder's local-v1 priority.
